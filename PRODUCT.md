# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-hosted operators who use YYB Go with QingLong and need to scan a WeChat account, verify its saved identity, and call mini-program protocol endpoints.

## Product Purpose

Provide a compact local console for adding accounts, inspecting account identifiers and health, and testing supported protocol calls without exposing stored credentials.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technical, calm.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style layouts, decorative dashboards, hidden identifiers, and success states that omit the data needed for the next operational step.

## Design Principles

- Show the saved account identifiers immediately after scanning.
- Keep account state and protocol actions easy to verify.
- Prefer familiar controls and explicit error states.
- Present accounts as compact cards with restrained spacing; preserve the card layout when refining density.
- Treat account credentials and identifiers as sensitive operational data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
